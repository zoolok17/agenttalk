## Blockers

### 1. Concurrent close mutations could overwrite newer state

- **Severity:** Blocker.
- **Original risk:** ack, counter, reopen, and publish used stale
  load-evaluate-save state. A concurrent mutation could be lost, a
  delete/recreate ABA could satisfy a stale updater, and publish could persist a
  stale GO or leave an unrecoverable barrier seam.
- **Exact fix commits:**
  `c0b4f381bbae554d707556f298f24d428c6a06e4`,
  `ff9c26b184614e299a8cc3ed85f4d2439be5758f`,
  `8f109aa18f523bfe1b6e557aca1c4488f7958095`, and
  `40989df304384d0c585c27aeff510fc419c7cc9f`.
- **Source verification:** `src/agenttalk/close.py` provides per-close
  transactions, mandatory generation plus immutable instance checks, exclusive
  create, checked replace, and legacy in-lock upgrade. `src/agenttalk/cli.py`
  uses the transaction boundary for mutations and resumes bound barrier
  send/stamp work idempotently.
- **Regression evidence:**
  `test_concurrent_close_saves_reject_one_stale_generation`,
  `test_delete_recreate_rejects_stale_instance_at_same_generation`,
  `test_cli_publish_serializes_against_ack_and_counter`,
  `test_cli_publish_barrier_send_failure_resumes_exactly_once`,
  `test_cli_publish_barrier_stamp_failure_resumes_existing_message`, and
  `test_cli_concurrent_publish_barrier_retries_send_once`.
- **Status:** **FIXED at `40989df`**.
- **Residual risk:** conflicts intentionally fail closed and require callers to
  reload/retry. Close state plus barrier message is a recoverable protocol, not
  a cross-file ACID transaction. Non-cooperating same-user file edits remain
  outside the lock contract.

### 4. Unterminated JSONL tails could absorb later records

### 5. Supervisor state recovery was destructive and non-atomic

  `test_python_supervisor_state_recovers_only_from_validated_backup`,

- **Status:** **FIXED at `40989df`**.
- **Residual risk:** the backup may be one valid generation behind. Recovery is
  fail-closed persistence, not transactional synchronization with live OS
  processes.

## Major findings

### 6. No-worktree waiver authority was mutable

- **Severity:** Major.
- **Original risk:** mutable roster labels such as `operator_facing` or a lead
  role could authorize a no-worktree waiver and let unisolated evidence satisfy
  release-class consumption without an explicit immutable authority grant.
- **Integrated partial commit:**
  `4853f939a6bcd6fd3524e92f9fd62f8a85d64c69` records waiver metadata and rejects
  an ordinary developer or forged waiver.
- **Source verification exception:** at `40989df`,
  `worktree_waiver_authority` in `src/agenttalk/lanes.py` still resolves current
  `operator_facing` or `sole_lead` labels. `validate_delivery_artifact` accepts a
  `waived` artifact when `require_isolation=True`, and
  `_close_worktree_eval` in `src/agenttalk/cli.py` returns status `waived` rather
  than making it non-consumable. This does not support the supplied map's
  `advisory_only` claim.
- **Regression evidence present:**
  `test_cli_no_worktree_waiver_requires_lead_or_operator` and
  `test_cli_deliver_rejects_forged_no_worktree_waiver` prove the partial policy,
  not immutable waiver authority.
- **Status:** **PARTIAL / NOT VERIFIED AS FIXED at `40989df`**.
- **Residual risk:** the original mutable-authority problem remains for waived
  release-class artifacts. A reviewed fix must either introduce explicit
  audited immutable authority or make every no-worktree waiver advisory and
  non-consumable for release.

### 26. JSONL readers allocated the whole ledger

- **Severity:** Medium/low (unsplit in source map).

  knowledge, onboarding, and attention readers no longer call

- **Regression evidence:** `test_knowledge_reader_streams_without_path_read_text`,

  `test_attention_readers_stream_without_path_read_text`.
- **Status:** **FIXED at `40989df`**.
- **Residual risk:** one exceptionally large physical line is still allocated as

### 27. Onboarding string `false` became blocking `true`

- **Severity:** Medium/low (unsplit in source map).

- **Exact fix commit:**
  `24e25c5b4e07b26e4a44784338fd15de3cf89f2e`.
